How It Works

The Seamless Wholesale Process

Buying fabrics in bulk doesn't have to be complicated. Our straightforward 5-step procurement process gets bolts to your workshop faster.

Step 01

Browse Catalogue

Explore our ready inventory of pure cottons, rayns, linen, denims, and prints. Filter by material and GSM.

Step 02

Select Quality

Choose your desired fabric code, width, target meters, and pick shade codes from our color options.

Step 03

Enquire Directly

Hit 'Enquire on WhatsApp' or 'Send Email'. All product specs are pre-formatted automatically into your message.

Receive tiered rate cards, sample swatches if required, stock lot confirmations, and GST tax invoice estimate.

Confirm & Dispatch
